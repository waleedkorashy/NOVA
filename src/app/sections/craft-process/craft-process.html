1. 01

    Material first.

    Solid oak where a frame has to carry, ash where a curve has to stay true, walnut only where a face is visible. Grain sets the dimensions, and the drawing is cut to the board.

  2. 02

    Joinery, not hardware.

    Wedged tenons and through-mortises do the carrying, and are left visible from the side. A frame should come apart and go back together without a mark.

  3. 03

    A finish that can be repaired.

    Hard-wax oil worked in and left to cure between coats. Matte, so the timber still reads as timber and a damaged surface can be treated locally instead of sprayed again.
