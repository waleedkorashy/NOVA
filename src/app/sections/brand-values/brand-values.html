1. 01

    Proportion settles it.

    Line, seat height, leg spacing. When those three agree, a piece is finished — everything after that is subtraction.

  2. 02

    The joint stays visible.

    Wedged tenons and through-mortises, sized to be read from the side. A joint hidden behind a face is a joint that has not been thought about twice.

  3. 03

    It should age, not expire.

    Surfaces are chosen for how they wear. A piece earns its place by looking better in year five than it did on the day it arrived.
